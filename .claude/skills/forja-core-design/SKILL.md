---
name: forja-core-design
description: Develop a visual direction and inspect rendered compositions for open visual briefs; preserve approved designs.
---

Establish a visual direction for an open brief, or develop the existing approved direction when one exists. Start with the audience's concrete question and the content that must answer it. Pick a spatial organizing rule drawn from that content: the relationships between elements must do work, not merely decorate a conventional hero. Compare a few viable arrangements briefly, then draw one coherent proposal. Do not spend the deliverable on persuasive design prose.

Use the actual content and available assets. Decide what is dominant, secondary and quiet; give these distinctions visible scale, placement and spacing. Choose type for its role and available rendering, not novelty alone. Essential dates/actions must remain clear without waiting for decoration. Colour and motion support that hierarchy. No palette, serif, grid, radius or library is universally forbidden; judge whether it fits and whether the composition is interchangeable with unrelated products.

On mobile choose what reorganizes, what simplifies and what stays identifiable. Do not merely scale down a desktop poster. Before handoff, use rendered desktop/mobile evidence if available to inspect reading order, collisions, crop, density and useful space. If the controller supplies screenshots, read them and correct the actual drawing; a justification cannot resolve an overlap. Explain what motion would communicate and the static equivalent, but do not claim a still proves animation or accessibility.

Deliver the artifact and a concise decision record: organizing rule, essential mobile change, material asset/technology dependency and any unresolved visual weakness. Prefer one defensible direction to several weak variants. Stop when the bounded task is done; do not invent a brand system or another feature.
